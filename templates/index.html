ABOUT FK PHYSICAL ASSETS

FK Physical Assets is an independent consulting and facilitation firm specializing in physical and alternative assets. We work with clients seeking exposure to tangible assets by providing access to industry expertise, established international networks, and practical transaction support.

Our role extends beyond facilitating transactions, our role is not simply to facilitate transactions. We help clients understand available options, identify appropriate solutions, and connect with trusted counterparties across multiple jurisdictions.

While our primary focus remains precious metals, we also assist clients in navigating broader physical asset opportunities through our network of industry participants and specialist service providers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does FK Physical Assets do?

+

FK Physical Assets is an independent consulting and facilitation firm that assists clients with sourcing, introductions, transaction support, storage coordination, and logistics relating to physical and alternative assets.

Does FK Physical Assets sell precious metals directly?

+

FK Physical Assets primarily operates as a consulting and intermediary business. We assist clients in identifying opportunities and connecting with appropriate suppliers, service providers, and market participants.

Can you help arrange storage?

+

Yes. We can introduce clients to secure storage providers and vaulting facilities in selected jurisdictions around the world.

Can you assist with international logistics?

+

Yes. We work with specialist logistics providers and can assist with coordinating the transportation of physical assets where required.

Do you only work with precious metals?

+

No. While precious metals remain our primary area of focus, we also assist clients with other categories of physical and alternative assets through our established network of industry relationships.

Do you provide financial advice?

+

No. FK Physical Assets does not provide investment, legal, tax, or financial advice. Clients should seek advice from appropriately qualified professionals before making investment decisions.

How do I get started?

+

Contact our team to arrange an initial discussion. We will take the time to understand your objectives and determine how we may be able to assist.
