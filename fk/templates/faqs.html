What does FK Physical sell?

+

We sell physical gold and silver to qualifying clients through our United States operations.

Do you provide storage?

+

No. We do not operate a storage facility. If required, our team can introduce you to a separate storage provider.

Do you sell investment products?

+

No. We do not provide investment management services or sell securities or paper precious metals products.

Who can purchase?

+

All clients are required to complete our onboarding and compliance process before transacting.

How are transactions completed?

+

Transactions are completed through FK Physical LLC's United States operations. Clients must be willing and able to remit payment in accordance with our US payment instructions.

Can you help with storage in Panama?

+

For clients interested in secure storage in Panama, we may be able to introduce you to Fort Kobbe Management Corp., a separate Panama-based company.
